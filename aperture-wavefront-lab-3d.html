What is being computed

Incident wave at distance ρ from the source:

U₀ = (A/ρ) · exp[i(kρ − ωt)], k = 2π/λ

Field behind the sheet, from the Huygens–Fresnel–Kirchhoff integral over the open area, summed numerically with no Fresnel or Fraunhofer approximation:

U(P) = 1/(iλ) · ∬ K · A·exp[ik(ρ+r)] / (ρ·r) dx dy, K = ½ (cos θ_ρ + cos θ_r)

For an obstacle, Babinet's principle gives the field as the free wave minus the field of the matching hole. The sheet absorbs: reflections are left out.

The screen image and the field slice (the x–z plane through the axis) are the full diffraction result. The amber shells are selected crests drawn by straight-line rays, so they show the geometric shadow; compare them with the slice to see where diffraction departs from it. The slice compresses amplitude so weak fields stay visible. Lengths are in any one unit, for example µm.
