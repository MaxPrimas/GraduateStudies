What is being computed

Incident wave from the source at distance ρ:

U₀ = (A/ρ) · exp[i(kρ − ωt)], k = 2π/λ

Field behind S, from the Huygens–Fresnel–Kirchhoff integral over the open part of S. The aperture is uniform along y (slits), so the y integral is done by stationary phase and what remains is a line integral along the curve:

U(P) = e^(−iπ/4)/√λ · ∫ₛ K · A·exp[ik(ρ+r)] / √(ρ·r·(ρ+r)) dl
K = ½ (n̂·ŝ + n̂·r̂) obliquity factor, n̂ = normal of S

ρ runs source to S, r runs S to the field point. Opaque parts of S absorb: reflections are left out (Kirchhoff boundary conditions). Wave view compresses amplitude so the weak diffracted field stays visible. Lengths are in any one unit, for example µm. Dashed marks on the screen trace show the geometric shadow edges.
