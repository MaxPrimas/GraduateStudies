What is being computed

Any spin-½ density matrix is fixed by a real vector r with |r| ≤ 1, the Bloch vector, whose components are the expectation values of the Pauli matrices:

ρ = ½ (1 + r·σ), r = (⟨σx⟩, ⟨σy⟩, ⟨σz⟩)

A pure state cos(θ/2)|↑⟩ + e^(iφ) sin(θ/2)|↓⟩ sits on the surface at polar angle θ and azimuth φ. With the field along the unit vector n̂, the Hamiltonian and the equation of motion from iħ dρ/dt = [H, ρ] are

H = (ħω/2) n̂·σ ⟹ dr/dt = ω n̂ × r

so r rotates rigidly about n̂ at angular frequency ω. The component along the field is conserved and the perpendicular part traces a circle. The motion drawn here is the exact rotation, not a numerical integration.

Tr ρ² = ½ (1 + |r|²), S = −Σ p± ln p±, p± = ½ (1 ± |r|)

Unitary evolution never changes |r|, so purity and entropy stay fixed while the state precesses: a field alone cannot turn a mixed state into a pure one.
